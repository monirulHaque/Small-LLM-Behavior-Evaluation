\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated 30 small open language models, all of which run on an 8\,GB consumer
GPU, on nine user-facing behaviours: factual and social sycophancy, dark patterns,
over-refusal and refusal style, hedge faithfulness, clarification-seeking,
cognitive biases, explicit and implicit social bias, and verbosity. We added
capability controls and a controlled quantization ladder.
