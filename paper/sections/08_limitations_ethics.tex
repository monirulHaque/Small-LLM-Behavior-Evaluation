\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Small local judges.} Judged behaviours rely on 12--14B local judges, which
are noisier than frontier judges. We quantify this error by validating against hand
labels and reporting per-behaviour $\kappa$ (\cref{tab:judge-kappa}), and we mark
behaviours with low agreement as exploratory. The hand labels come from a single
annotator, the author. This is not a human-subjects study, and inter-annotator
agreement between several humans is not available.

\paragraph{Benchmark contamination.} BBQ, MMLU, TriviaQA and XSTest are likely to
appear in the training data of some models. Absolute levels should be interpreted
with caution. Our main conclusions rely on within-model contrasts (paired flips,
quantization deltas, template contrasts), which are less affected by contamination.

\paragraph{Single-turn and short multi-turn settings.} Except for \Bx{1} (two turns),
behaviours are measured in single-turn interactions. Anthropomorphic behaviours and
sycophancy can first appear only after several turns~\citep{ibrahim2025multiturn,hong2025sycon,laban2025lost}.
Our rates are therefore likely lower bounds for long conversations.

\paragraph{GGUF artefact heterogeneity.} GGUF builds differ by uploader, importance
matrix and \texttt{llama.cpp} version. Build differences can change behaviour, and
GGUF quantization can even be adversarially exploited~\citep{egashira2025mindgap}. We
record SHA-256 hashes and the build, but our results describe these specific
artefacts. Other builds with the same nominal label may behave differently.

\paragraph{Model sample and English only.} The 30 models are a convenience sample,
limited by an 8\,GB GPU and by availability in October 2026, and they are not a random
sample of all small models. All prompts are in English. Bias and safety effects of
quantization are known to be larger in some non-English settings~\citep{alhakim2026preserving,ferrara2026quantibias}.

\paragraph{Decoding settings.} Greedy decoding is reproducible, but it does not show
the sampling variability that users experience with default chat temperatures. Only
\Bx{5} uses sampling.

\paragraph{Statistical power across models.} With 30 models, cross-model correlation
and PCA analyses are descriptive. Confirmatory claims rely on item-level mixed
models with thousands of observations.

\section{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}
This study involves no human participants. All datasets are public, and we follow
their licences (ELEPHANT uses public Reddit posts as released by its authors). Some
prompts (OR-Bench-Toxic, XSTest unsafe, DarkBench harmful generation) ask for harmful
content. We do not publish harmful generations verbatim in the paper, and we release
them only under the source datasets' terms with a content warning. Bias probes
necessarily mention demographic groups. We report aggregate results and avoid
reproducing stereotyped outputs. Several models carry non-commercial licences, and
our use is non-commercial research. Finally, the study runs on a single consumer
GPU. We report total energy use (GPU-hours $\times$ measured board power $=$ \TBD\,kWh).
